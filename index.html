About Aesthetic Furniture

Aesthetic Furniture designs and builds furniture for homes and workplaces. Every piece starts as a sketch in one of our design offices, is built by hand in our workshop and is tested in our showroom before it goes into production.

Materials

We work with birch plywood, solid oak, recycled steel and wool upholstery, and we choose timber from responsibly managed forests.
