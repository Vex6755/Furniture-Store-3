News

Product launches, exhibitions and updates from Aesthetic Furniture.

2 October 2026

The Alba armchair joins our autumn collection

Designed by Marco Bellini, Alba has a deep, rounded seat, wool upholstery and slim black steel legs. It is available in four colours and is on display in all of our showrooms from this week.

18 September 2026

Showrooms now open on Saturdays

By popular request, our Berlin and Munich showrooms are open from 10:00 to 16:00 on Saturdays. Our team will help you plan a room, compare finishes and arrange delivery.

5 September 2026

Lumen mirror wins a regional design award

The jury praised Lumen for its calm proportions and its solid oak frame, which is made from offcuts that would otherwise go to waste. Designer Tobias Hofer accepted the award on behalf of the team.

20 August 2026

New partnership with a certified oak supplier

From this autumn, all of our solid oak comes from forests certified for responsible management. The change covers every product made in our workshop, with no change to prices.
